We do not ask for your name, email address or other personal information to play our games. Game progress and settings are stored on your device.

Advertising

Our games may show ads from third-party ad networks, such as Google AdMob. These partners may collect information like your device's advertising identifier, IP address and general usage data to show and measure ads. On iPhone, you'll be asked for permission before your advertising identifier is used for tracking, and you can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
